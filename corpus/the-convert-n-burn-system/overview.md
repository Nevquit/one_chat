
# Overview

Convert n’ Burn is Wanchain’s Bridge Fees, Discounts and Benefits system. It was designed with two primary goals in mind: to offset the on-chain costs incurred by the Wanchain Bridge and to create value for the entire Wanchain ecosystem. It achieves both these goals by standardising fees on [WanBridge](https://docs.wanchain.org/products/wanbridge.md), converting the bulk of collected fees to [WAN coins](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md), and redistributing the value throughout the entirety of the Wanchain ecosystem.

By aligning every component of the Wanchain ecosystem, including but not limited to Wanchain’s industry-leading [cross-chain infrastructure](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wanchain-bridge-node-group.md), the [Wanchain L1 blockchain](broken://pages/ZiJoxkixshZnOyHGATRR), the [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) and the Wanchain community, Convert n’ Burn is a shining example of the kinds of synergies that form when the whole works in unison.
