
# IntentX

XIntents is Wanchain’s&#x20;

web-based staking platform. It houses a variety of infrastructure-supporting staking opportunities. Using XStake, users can easily help secure the Wanchain Layer 1 blockchain by delegating WAN to Wanchain PoS Validator Nodes participating in Galaxy Consensus. This staking option encourages participation in the Wanchain Layer 1 blockchain and creates a more democratic and resilient network. Users can also support all of Wanchain’s cross-chain solutions by delegating WAN to Wanchain Bridge Nodes. Both these staking options yield daily rewards and bolster the robustness and security of the Wanchain ecosystem.
