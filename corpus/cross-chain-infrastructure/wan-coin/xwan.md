
# xWAN

xWAN is a [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) derivative. It is an escrowed token, corresponding to staked [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md). It has several use cases throughout the Wanchain Ecosystem, including:

* Farming rewards on [XFlows](https://docs.wanchain.org/products/xflows.md)
* Rewards for [Bridge-to-Earn](https://docs.wanchain.org/products/wanbridge/bridge-to-earn.md)
* Staking on [XStake](https://docs.wanchain.org/products/xstake.md) \[in development]

[WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) can be instantly and freely converted into xWAN at a 1:1 ratio using <https://xflows.wanchain.org/>. The xWAN contract address is: 0x2eA407Aa69be7367BF231E76B51fab9eC436766c.

Redeeming xWAN for [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) involves a linear vesting period, selected by the user. The longer the vesting period, the more [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) is received:

* The minimum vesting period of 0 days will provide a 1:0.25 ratio
* The maximum vesting period of 90 days will provide a 1:1 ratio

Users can cancel the redemption at any time before the selected vesting period ends. Cancelling will return the full xWAN amount to the user.

Redeem xWAN at [https://xflows.wanchain.org/vesting/](https://xflows.wanchain.org/vesting)
