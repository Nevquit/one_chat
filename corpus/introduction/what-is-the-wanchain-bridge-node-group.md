
# What is the Wanchain Bridge Node Group?

The Wanchain Bridge Node Group is a collection of decentralised, permissionless Bridge Nodes that are rotated and re-elected monthly. Bridge Nodes use a combination of Secure Multiparty Computation (sMPC) and Shamir’s Secret Sharing cryptography to execute cross-chain transactions, secure cross-chain assets, and transfer messages and arbitrary data between EVM and non-EVM blockchains.
