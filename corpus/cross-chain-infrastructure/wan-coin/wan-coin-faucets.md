## Automatic cross-chain faucet

Mainnet: The automatic cross-chain faucet ensures that anyone who completes a cross-chain transaction to the [Wanchain L1 blockchain](broken://pages/ZiJoxkixshZnOyHGATRR) will always have at least 0.0005 [WAN coin](https://docs.wanchain.org/cross-chain-infrastructure/wan-coin.md) — enough to cover several transactions.
