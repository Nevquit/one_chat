
# What is XFlows?

Housed within WanBridge, XFlows is Wanchain’s canonical bridge. XFlows uses decentralised liquidity pools to enable native-to-native cross-chain asset transfers. This protocol currently supports 4 assets, BTC, ETH, USDC and USDT, across more than a dozen blockchains. These 4 assets represent more than 90% of all crypto transactions.
